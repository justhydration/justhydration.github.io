Privacy Policy

Fruity Tumble · Effective October 29, 2026
The short version: Fruity Tumble has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your game stays on your own iPhone. We (the developer) never receive any of your information.

Who we are

Fruity Tumble is made by Trent Howard, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Fruity Tumble game for iPhone does with information. If you have questions, contact us at justhydration.app@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a few things on your iPhone:

  • the game you're in the middle of, so you can continue it later;
  • your best score, top scores and game stats;
  • your coins, boosters, stars, daily rewards, missions, Fruit Book and the rooms you've decorated;
  • the skins and themes you've picked, and your settings (sound, vibration, aim line).

This information never leaves your device. We do not run any servers for the game and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your own Apple settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No purchases: coins and stars are earned only by playing and have no real-money value.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Deleting your information

Settings → Reset progress in the game clears your scores, coins, boosters, saved game and other progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Fruity Tumble is suitable for all ages. Because the game does not collect any personal information, we do not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.
