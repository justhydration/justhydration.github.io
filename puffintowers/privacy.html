Privacy Policy

Puffin Towers · Effective November 5, 2026
The short version: Puffin Towers has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your game stays on your own iPhone. We (the developer) never receive any of your information.

Who we are

Puffin Towers is made by Trent Howard, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Puffin Towers game for iPhone does with information. If you have questions, contact us at justhydration.app@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a few things on your iPhone:

  • your best scores (including each day's best in Daily Light), your awards and your game stats;
  • your coins and upgrades;
  • the messages you've found in bottles;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration) and which tips you've already seen.

This information never leaves your device. We do not run any servers for the game and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your own Apple settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No purchases: coins are earned only by playing and have no real-money value.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or motion sensors.

Deleting your information

Settings → Start Over in the game clears your scores, coins, upgrades, awards, messages and stats. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Puffin Towers is suitable for all ages. Because the game does not collect any personal information, we do not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: justhydration.app@gmail.com
