Habit Duo

A calm tally counter for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Count anything you do every day: glasses of water, reps, pages, good deeds. Group your tallies into sets, tap to count, set goals and automatic resets, and see your progress in History, on widgets and on your wrist.

Common questions

How do I restore Habit Duo Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Habit Duo, go to Settings › Habit Duo Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro can also be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Habit Duo Pro › Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the year you've paid for. The Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

My sets don't appear on my other iPhone or iPad.

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and iCloud Sync on in Habit Duo (Settings › iCloud Sync, which shows when it last synced). New changes can take a minute to arrive.

A widget shows an old number.

Open Habit Duo once and widgets update straight away. Counting with a widget's + button updates it within a few seconds.

How do I use Habit Duo on Apple Watch?

The Apple Watch app comes with Habit Duo Pro. It shows the sets that have "Show on Apple Watch" turned on (in each set's options), and counts even when your iPhone is out of reach.

How do automatic resets work?

Each tally can go back to its starting value every day at a time you choose, every week or every month. Edit the tally and pick a reset under Automatic Reset. Every reset is kept in History.

Does Habit Duo collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
