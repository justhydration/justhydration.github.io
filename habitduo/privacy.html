Privacy Policy

Habit-Duo - Habit Tracker · Effective November 9, 2026
The short version: Habit Duo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your tallies stay on your own devices and in your own iCloud. We (the developer) never receive your data.

Who we are

Habit Duo is made by Trent Howard, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Habit Duo app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch does with information. If you have questions, contact us at justhydration.app@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores what you enter: your sets and tallies, their history of changes, and the app's settings.

  • This information is saved on your device. It is shared with Habit Duo's widgets, Live Activities and Control Center controls on the same device, and with Habit Duo on your Apple Watch using Apple's built-in connection between your devices.
  • We do not operate any servers for the app, and this information is never sent to us.
  • Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups, according to your own Apple settings.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud Sync is on (Settings › iCloud Sync in the app), your sets, tallies and history are also kept in your own private iCloud database, provided by Apple, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Only you can see this data; we cannot access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Intelligence

Habit Duo's Shortcuts and Siri actions run on your device on your own tallies. "Describe a Set" (on devices with Apple Intelligence) turns your description into a set using Apple's on-device model; your text is not sent to us.

Purchases

Habit Duo Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. App Store sales reports we receive from Apple do not identify you.

Your choices: export and delete

  • Export: you can share a set as a file, or export its history as a CSV spreadsheet. Files are shared only where you choose to send them.
  • Delete: deleting a set removes it and its history. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Data in iCloud can be removed in your device's Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Habit Duo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
