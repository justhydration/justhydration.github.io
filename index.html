Common questions

How do I restore Just Hydration Plus on a new phone?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Just Hydration Plus → Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Just Hydration and make sure notifications are allowed. In the app, reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal; you can change this in Settings → Reminders.

The widget or Apple Watch shows an old total.

Open Just Hydration on your iPhone once; widgets and the Watch update right away. Widgets also refresh on their own every so often.

How is my daily goal calculated?

About 33 ml per kilogram of body weight, plus extra for activity and climate. Plus subscribers can also have it raised automatically on hot days and workout days. You can set your own goal in Settings → Daily goal. This is general guidance, not medical advice.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your devices. See the Privacy Policy.
