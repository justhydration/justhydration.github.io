Privacy Policy

Hungry Highness · Effective November 3, 2026
The short version: Hungry Highness has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your game stays on your own iPhone. We (the developer) never receive any of your information.

Who we are

Hungry Highness is made by Trent Howard, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Hungry Highness game for iPhone does with information. If you have questions, contact us at justhydration.app@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a few things on your iPhone:

  • your best score in each mode, your awards and your game stats;
  • your coins, power-ups and upgrades;
  • the cakes you've designed in the cake editor;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, steering) and which tips you've already seen.

This information never leaves your device. We do not run any servers for the game and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your own Apple settings.

Motion

If you choose to steer the King by tilting your phone, the game reads the phone's motion sensor while the King is flying. This is used only to steer, right there and then; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No purchases: coins are earned only by playing and have no real-money value.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Deleting your information

Settings → Start over in the game clears your coins, scores, power-ups, upgrades, awards, stats and cakes. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Hungry Highness is suitable for all ages. Because the game does not collect any personal information, we do not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.
