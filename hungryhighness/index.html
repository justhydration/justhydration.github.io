How do I play?

A cake slides back and forth over the chef's golden tray. Tap anywhere to drop it, and build the tallest tower you can. When the stacking ends, the King blasts off up through your tower: drag left and right (or tilt your phone, if you pick that in the pause menu) to steer him into the cakes. You can see the steps any time with the ? button on the home screen.

What are the three modes?

Classic Mode is endless and keeps getting faster; drop three cakes and it's over. Time Mode gives you one minute. Cake Mode gives you 50 cakes to stack as well as you can.

What are the bonuses?

Land a cake right in the middle for an Accuracy Bonus or right at the edge for an Edge Bonus; bonuses in a row multiply your points. Only just miss a bomb for a Danger Bonus, and bonk a bird with a falling cake for a Bird Bonus.

How do I get coins?

Every round pays coins from your score, coins the King grabs in the feast, golden cakes and awards. Spend them in the Shop on power-ups and upgrades. There's nothing to buy with real money: everything is earned by playing.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone. Your coins, best scores, awards, stats and cakes you've designed are saved automatically. They stay on this iPhone only: they don't sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes them.

I can't hear any sounds

Hungry Highness follows your iPhone's ring/silent switch, so flip it to ring. Music and sound can also be switched on and off with the buttons at the top of the home screen, and in Settings.

How do I start over?

Home screen → Settings → Start over. This clears your coins, scores, power-ups, upgrades, awards, stats and cakes. Sound, music and vibration settings stay as they are.
