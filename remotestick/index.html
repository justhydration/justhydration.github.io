RemoteStick

Remote control for your TV, right on your iPhone

RemoteStick turns your iPhone into a remote for the TVs in your home: Fire TV, Samsung, Roku, LG and Google TV (Android TV). It finds them on your Wi-Fi, pairs once, and gives you arrows, a touchpad, your TV's apps, typing, volume and power.

Common questions

My TV isn't showing up.

Turn the TV on with its own remote, and make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network) with no VPN on either. Check that RemoteStick is allowed under iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. You can also tap "Type the address instead" and enter the TV's address from its network settings.

How do I pair?

  • Fire TV: type the 4-digit code the TV shows.
  • Google TV / Android TV: type the 6-character code the TV shows. If no code appears, update the "Android TV Remote Service" app on the TV.
  • Samsung: choose Allow when the TV asks about RemoteStick. If it never asks, look in the TV's Settings → General → External Device Manager → Device Connection Manager.
  • LG: accept the request on the TV. On older LG TVs, turn on Settings → Network → LG Connect Apps.
  • Roku: nothing to pair. If buttons don't work, set Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps → Network access to Default.

Why doesn't voice search show up for my TV?

Hold-to-talk works with Fire TV, which accepts a phone's microphone. Samsung, Roku, LG and Google TV don't take voice from other phone apps, so RemoteStick shows volume buttons there instead.

Typing doesn't appear in YouTube.

Select a search box on the TV first. Some apps, like YouTube on Fire TV, use their own keyboard that ignores phone typing; use voice search there instead.

The power button doesn't turn my TV on.

A TV that's fully off isn't on the network, so no phone can reach it. Fire TV, Roku TVs and Google TVs switch on from standby. Samsung and LG TVs switch on from the app only while they're still on the network in standby (their "Quick Start" option helps); otherwise use the TV's own power button.

How do I restore RemoteStick Pro on a new phone?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Does the app collect my data?

No. RemoteStick only talks to your TV over your home Wi-Fi. See the Privacy Policy.
