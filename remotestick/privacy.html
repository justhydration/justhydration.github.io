Privacy Policy

RemoteStick: TV Remote Control · Effective November 3, 2026
The short version: RemoteStick has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It talks to your TV over your home Wi-Fi and nothing else. We (the developer) never receive your data.

Who we are

RemoteStick is made by Trent Howard, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the RemoteStick app for iPhone does with information. If you have questions, contact us at justhydration.app@gmail.com.

Your home network

To find your TVs, RemoteStick looks for them on the Wi-Fi network your iPhone is connected to (this is why iPhone asks to let it "find devices on your local network"). The buttons you press, the text you type and the apps you open are sent straight from your iPhone to the TV you picked. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your iPhone

  • The TVs you've paired: their names, network addresses and type (such as Fire TV or Samsung).
  • The pairing key each TV handed over, and the certificate RemoteStick uses to pair with Google TVs. These are kept in the iPhone's encrypted Keychain. iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted; they only work for your own TVs on your own network.
  • Each TV's list of apps (with their pictures), your favorite apps and your settings.
  • For troubleshooting, the last app list each TV sent, stored in the app's own files. It is never sent anywhere automatically.

We do not operate any servers for the app, and none of this is sent to us. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups according to your own Apple settings.

Microphone (Fire TV voice search)

RemoteStick uses the microphone only while you hold its microphone button. What you say is streamed straight to your Fire TV, which handles it with Alexa, just like the voice button on its own remote. RemoteStick doesn't record, keep or send your voice anywhere else. You can turn microphone access off at any time in iPhone Settings → RemoteStick.

Your TV and its maker

What you do on your TV is handled by the TV's own software and its maker's services (Amazon, Samsung, Roku, LG or Google), under their own privacy policies. For example, voice requests on a Fire TV go to Amazon's Alexa. Some TVs point to app pictures stored on their maker's servers; your iPhone downloads those pictures directly, like any web page would.

Purchases

RemoteStick Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device to unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. App Store sales reports we receive from Apple do not identify you.

Deleting your information

In the app, Settings → Manage lets you forget a TV, which deletes its pairing key and saved apps. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored. To stop a TV from accepting RemoteStick, remove it from the TV's own list of connected devices or phone apps.

Children

RemoteStick is not directed to children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
